# Exit Strategy Cleaning Marketplace and Legal Roadmap

This is an internal, non-public roadmap. It describes possible future product work and does not state that a marketplace or subscription platform is currently active.

## Product direction

Reckley Ventures may develop technology that helps customers discover, request, book, schedule, communicate about, and keep records for cleaning services. Independent cleaning professionals would control their own businesses, service areas, availability, rates, and customer relationships. Platform tools could support discovery, booking, scheduling, service-area and rate settings, communications, invoices, records, and support.

Development, testing, market activation, pricing, and availability remain undecided.

## Future legal and policy set

Before launch, create and review separate documents for:

- Customer Marketplace Terms
- Provider Agreement
- Subscription Terms and provider software fees
- Booking, quote, and acceptance rules
- Cancellation, rescheduling, no-access, and lockout rules
- Payment, refund, chargeback, and payout rules
- Promotions, referral, and discount rules
- Property access, keys, alarms, and entry-code handling
- Service-photo and condition-documentation rules
- Provider screening and verification disclosures
- Insurance and provider-standard requirements
- Damage, claims, re-clean, complaints, and service guarantees
- Dispute resolution, governing law, and limitation-of-liability terms
- Account deletion and data-request procedures
- Marketplace privacy policy and data-retention schedule
- SMS, email, and marketing-consent terms
- Acceptable-use and prohibited-conduct policy

## Decisions required before implementation

The roadmap does not resolve worker classification, provider independence, subscription prices, marketplace fees, insurance requirements, screening scope, payment processors, tax handling, payouts, customer funds, liability allocation, service guarantees, cancellation/refund treatment, or dispute handling. Each decision requires business confirmation and legal review before product claims or public terms are published.
